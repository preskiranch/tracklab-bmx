# BMX bike mechanical audio

`bmx-bike-mechanics.mp3` uses the Freesound preview of:

- “BMX bike pedaling and wheel hub spinning or fishing reel”
- Author: byjoshberry
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/byjoshberry/sounds/460345/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

TrackLab uses separate loop regions from the recording for pedaling and
freewheel/coasting. Playback rate, volume, stereo position, and loop start are
adjusted independently for each rider.
