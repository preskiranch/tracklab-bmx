# TrackLab race ambience

TrackLab builds 128 race-by-race ambience profiles from two overlapping Mixkit
recordings: an always-on trackside crowd bed and a longer active sports-crowd
layer. Each profile changes both starting points and adds a very small
playback-rate variation so consecutive races do not repeat the same soundscape.

The sports-crowd layer is kept inside its active 4–68 second section. Its quiet
intro/outro is never used during a race, and the trackside bed remains audible
under cadence, live commentary, and any rise or fall in cheering.

The recordings are used under the Mixkit Sound Effects Free License. They were
not extracted from a USA BMX broadcast. The older short applause and chant
assets remain in the repository for provenance but are no longer looped as race
ambience because their fades created audible quiet gaps.
