# Global Bike Shop Directory data notice

The bundled bike-shop catalog is derived from the **Overture Maps Places**
release `2026-08-19.0`, filtered to the current exact taxonomy categories
`bike_store` and `bike_repair_maintenance`.

Overture Maps data is made available under the CDLA-Permissive-2.0 license and
other compatible source licenses, including Apache-2.0 and CC0-1.0 for some
contributed sources. Required source and attribution details are maintained by
the Overture Maps Foundation:

- https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/
- https://docs.overturemaps.org/guides/places/
- https://overturemaps.org/

The complete CDLA-Permissive-2.0 text is included in
`CDLA-Permissive-2.0.txt`. The complete Apache-2.0 text is included in
`Apache-2.0.txt`. The upstream Foursquare attribution and TrackLab's change
notice are included in `Foursquare-NOTICE.txt`.

TrackLab applies a deterministic confidence threshold of `0.50`, excludes
records marked closed or permanently closed, and merges exact-name records
within 50 meters to reduce duplicate pins. The catalog was modified on
2026-09-01. TrackLab does not represent the catalog as exhaustive or guarantee
that a listed business is currently open.
Live OpenStreetMap bicycle-shop and bicycle-repair records are used as a second,
independently attributed source and may enrich the bundled catalog.
